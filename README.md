# Arc Weave

Weave abilities and pet commands into your swing rhythm, in **WoW Forever**.

Two jobs, one addon. `/arcweave` opens the options.

## Next Melee Weave

- **Ticks on the swing timer** - your next-melee abilities (Raptor Strike, Heroic Strike, Maul, Cleave) drawn on Blizzard's swing bar, so you can see when each comes back and on which swing it lands. Gold = back during this swing, amber +N = N swings out, green = ready, blue = queued, red = further out.
- **Off-hand lane** - the same ticks on the off-hand bar when you dual-wield.
- **Queue a next-attack ability** - pick the ability to queue (Raptor Strike, say) and then pick which of your abilities arm it. Press Mongoose Bite or drop a trap, and Raptor Strike is queued for your next swing at the same time. It stays queued even if you spam the button.

## Pet Weave

- **Send your pet from your own buttons** - pick the abilities that should also send your pet at your target, on their keybinds and on mouse clicks. The ability itself still casts normally.
- **Stays out of the way in vehicles and pet battles** - your keybinds go back to normal there automatically.

## How picking works

You pick abilities by clicking them on your action bars. Picks follow the **ability**, not the slot: move it to another button and it keeps working, and a second copy on another bar works too. Small corner markers show what each picked button does.

Needs Blizzard's swing timer turned on for the tick display.
